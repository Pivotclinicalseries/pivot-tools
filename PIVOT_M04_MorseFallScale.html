Clinical Pearl

The highest-value question is mental status — specifically whether the resident overestimates their own ability. The resident who says "I can get up fine" but cannot is your highest-risk patient. They will not call for help because they genuinely believe they don't need it. Your care plan must address this neurological reality directly — not with education, but with environmental and routine modifications.
