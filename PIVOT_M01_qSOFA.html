Clinical Pearl

qSOFA was designed for bedside use without labs. In SNF where labs take 24–48 hours, qSOFA gives you an actionable score in 30 seconds. A score of 2 means: call the provider now and say "Her qSOFA is 2 — I am concerned for sepsis." Those exact words change how fast the provider responds.
